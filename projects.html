Creature Evolver

Build a creature out of joints, bones and muscles, then let a neural network grow its own structure until it learns to run, jump, climb or swim. A body editor, a lab where evolution runs live with the brain graph drawn beside the creature, and a race mode that puts two trained genomes head to head over 10 kilometres. Pure JavaScript, no dependencies.

MathVoid

A first-person puzzle game in an infinite black void where the only tool you have is mathematics. Open the console, type a function, and it materialises as solid walkable geometry. A sloped line becomes a ramp. A parabola becomes a valley bridge. A sine wave becomes stairs you can climb. Levels build themselves in code - no node wiring.

Local AI Python

DIANA

A voice assistant that runs 100% offline on my own hardware. Push-to-talk into Whisper on the GPU, through a custom Ollama model, out through a Kokoro voice on the CPU. She starts speaking before she has finished thinking, remembers the conversation, and nothing ever leaves the machine.

Steam Wheel

Game night, solved. Spin a wheel built from your real Steam library - but the good part is that it reads your Steam Family ownership, so the wheel only offers games everybody in the room can actually launch. Local web app, double-click to run.

A prime sieve written in CUDA to find out how much faster the GPU really is than the CPU at brute-force number crunching. Mostly an excuse to learn how to write a kernel and think about memory coalescing.
