02 · Twin-stick roguelike

Timeless

A twin-stick roguelike where the clock is the health bar. You don’t lose hit points, you lose seconds — and when they run out, so do you. Built from nothing in Python: my own engine, my own room editor, and a meta-progression system shaped like the night sky.

In developmentRoguelikePythonCustom engine
43,000lines of code
94upgrades
  • Time is the health barTake a hit, lose seconds. Kill something, buy them back.
  • The run map is a constellationBetween rooms you pick your path across a star chart.
  • Hand-drawn artMy girlfriend hand drew all of the art in the game.
