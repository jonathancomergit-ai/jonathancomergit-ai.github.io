Pip, my personal assistant

Last updated October 14, 2026

Pip is a personal assistant I built for myself. It runs on my own home server, and the only Google accounts that ever sign in to it are my own. It isn’t offered to anyone else.

What it reads

With read-only permission, my Gmail messages and my Google Calendar events, so it can answer my questions (“anything due this week?”) and remind me about things.

Where it lives

On my own server. Its chat history and settings stay there, and its Google sign-in tokens are kept in a private file on that server.

Who else sees it

To write an answer, Pip sends the relevant bits (like an email’s subject and a short preview, or an event’s title and time) to Anthropic’s Claude API, under Anthropic’s API terms. Nothing goes anywhere else.

What it never does

It never sells, shares or advertises with any of it. It doesn’t send email and it doesn’t delete anything.

Turning it off

Access can be removed any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google’s rules

Pip’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
