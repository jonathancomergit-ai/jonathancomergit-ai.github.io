About

Hey, I’m Jonjoe1001.

I make games and the systems that hold them up. Most of what I build starts as a question I could not stop thinking about — can a neural network figure out how to walk if I never tell it what walking is? — and turns into a project somewhere between a weekend and a year later.

My first shipped game, Timeless, is a twin-stick roguelike I built from scratch in Python: custom engine, custom room editor, three bosses, and a full Steam integration with achievements and leaderboards. Everything in it, I had to figure out first.

What I work with

  • Python — game engines, tooling, automation, local AI pipelines
  • JavaScript — simulations that run in the browser with zero dependencies
  • Godot 4 — 3D, procedural geometry, GDScript
  • Java & C — coursework, CUDA experiments, and things that need to be fast
  • Self-hosting — TrueNAS, local services, keeping my own data

Where I am headed

More games, shipped more often. Better at 3D. And a growing pile of simulations that exist mostly to satisfy my own curiosity about how things move.

Get in touch

The fastest way to reach me is email. I am always up for talking about game architecture, physics, or why your engine is slow.
