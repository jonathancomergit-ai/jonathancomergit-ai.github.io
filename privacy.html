Privacy

What this site collects

Last updated October 7, 2026

Short version: very little. There are no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no tracking across other sites, and I never sell or share anything. This is a personal site run by one person in the US. Here’s everything, in plain English.

If you use the contact form

The form sends your name, email address and message to Formspree, a form service that emails it on to my Gmail inbox. I use it only to read and reply to you. Formspree also records basic technical details like your IP address to block spam, under its own privacy policy. Your message then lives in my email like any other email, until I delete it.

Visitor stats

I count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It sets no cookies and doesn’t store your IP address. It records which page you viewed, the site that linked you here, your browser and screen size, and your rough country. It also counts a few events: downloads, links to other sites, the email button, the contact form being sent, reaching the end of a post, and visits to pages that don’t exist (with the page that linked there). None of it identifies you, and ad blockers usually stop it entirely.

Fonts

The site’s fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any web request, that means Google’s servers see your IP address and browser when the page loads.

Saved on your device

Only one thing, and only if you ask for it: switching motion: off in the footer saves that choice in your browser’s local storage so it sticks. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Downloads

Wheelhouse runs entirely on your own computer. Your Steam keys stay on your PC and only go to Steam; I never see them or anything else it does.

Links to other sites

Links to YouTube, TikTok, Discord, itch.io, GitHub and Steam take you to sites with their own privacy policies, which I don’t control.

Kids

This site isn’t aimed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their information. If you’re under 13, please don’t send your name or email through the form. If I learn a message came from a child under 13, I delete it.

Getting your info deleted

Want me to delete a message you sent, or have a question about any of this? Reach me through the contact page and I’ll take care of it. If this page ever changes, the date at the top changes too.
