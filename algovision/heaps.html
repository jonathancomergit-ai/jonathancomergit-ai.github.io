Heaps & priority queues

A heap answers one question instantly: what's the smallest thing in here? It looks like a tree but lives in a plain array, and it stays tidy with two tiny moves: bubble up and sink down. It's what hospitals, schedulers and Dijkstra use to pick "who's next".

Why not just keep a sorted list?

        
A heap is only half sorted: each parent beats its children, and that's all. Doing less work is the point.
