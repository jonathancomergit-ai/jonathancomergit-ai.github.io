Why I made this

I learn by seeing

Most explanations of code are walls of text. "Merge sort splits the list in half, recursively..." Okay, but what does that look like? I'm a visual person. I didn't really get these ideas until I could watch them happen.

So I built the thing I wanted when I was learning: every concept as something you can watch, step through one move at a time, and break on purpose. No sign-up, no video to sit through. Just press play.
