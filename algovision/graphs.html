Graphs are everywhere

        
Spot the nodes and the edges, and a hard-looking problem becomes one of these.

        

          

            
Real thingNodesEdgesQuestion → algorithm

            
              
Social networkPeopleFriendships"How many handshakes away?" → BFS

              
Maps appJunctionsRoads, weighted by minutes"Fastest route?" → Dijkstra / A*

              
Course catalogueCourses"A unlocks B" arrows"What order can I take them?" → topological sort

              
A maze or game levelSquaresOpen sides"Can I get out?" → DFS or BFS (see Pathfinding)

              
The webPagesLinks (one way)"Crawl everything" → BFS with a seen set
