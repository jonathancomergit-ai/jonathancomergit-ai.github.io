Limitless Lab is open: 20 free things to poke

2026-10-09  ·  LIMITLESS LAB  ·  4 min read

Have you ever had a list of “little things I’d love to build” that just keeps getting longer? Mine was HUGE. Tiny games, physics toys, that one tool I keep googling for. So I stopped adding to the list and built a place to put them all. Say hi to Limitless Lab!

It’s 20 free things that run right in your browser. No installs, no accounts, no ads. And it works on your phone (honestly, that’s where I test it first).

Three wings

  • 🎮 Arcade: small games. Timeless Mini (yes, a tiny Timeless!), Orbit Sling, Daily Grid, Slime Dash, Gravity Golf and Brick Breaker+.
  • 🔬 Lab: science and maths you can poke. A neural network learning live, Fourier drawings, chaos pendulums, flocking birds, gravity, waves and an endless fractal zoom.
  • 🧰 Workshop: free tools for makers. Shrink images, make game sound effects, build colour palettes, slice sprite sheets, make app icons and QR codes.

Private on purpose

This was a big one for me. Nothing you do ever leaves your device.

  • The tools never upload your files. Your images get squished right there in your browser.
  • Saves stay on your device, and you can export, import or delete them whenever you want.
  • The pages can’t load stuff from other sites. The only exception is a cookie-free visit counter, so I know if anyone shows up!

How it got built (the honest bit)

I’ll be real with you: I didn’t hand-type all 20 of these. I built them with an AI crew. I use Claude Code cloud sessions, which are like little coders that live in the cloud. The loop went like this:

  1. We brainstorm ideas and I pick the ones I like.
  2. Each idea gets a short plan with a checklist of what “done” means.
  3. I send one cloud session per wing off to build overnight, one item at a time.
  4. In the morning every item has been tested on a phone-sized and a desktop-sized screen, with screenshots.
  5. I look at it all, play with it, and only then hit merge. About a minute later it’s live.

The wild part? It worked out to about $2 an item. That’s less than my coffee. (I still made the coffee.)

I went to bed with a list and woke up to a list of things I could actually play.

It wasn’t all magic. Throwing a bunch of browsers at it at once found a real bug, where a canvas tried to be a negative size (which is not a size). That’s why every item gets tested and looked at before it goes live.

What’s next

More items! On the list: Stack Tower, Snake Evolved, a Game of Life lab, a pathfinding visualiser, a regex tester and a pixel art scaler. I also want real thumbnails on the cards and a way to install it like an app so it works offline.

Go poke something and tell me what breaks, or what you want next, on Discord!
